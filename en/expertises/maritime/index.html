Role of FML

FML develops the administrative organisation, the oversight of operators and agents, the monitoring of obligations and the coordination with authorities.

Separation of roles

Administering a flag, fishing rights and industrial value creation are three distinct activities. Public responsibilities rest with the competent authorities and are kept separate from commercial interests.
