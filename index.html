The Group

Three pillars, one direction

FML Group was built on a simple conviction: that finance, the sea, and the supply chain are not separate businesses but one continuous chain of trust. We back the ventures that need capital, we govern the waters that carry the region's trade, and we move what industry depends on — port to mine, mine to cement plant. Mauritius is home. West Africa is where we grow.
